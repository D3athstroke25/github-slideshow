Kinboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Kinboard is a family organizer. This policy explains what it stores and why.

What we store

Only what your family types into the app: names you give to family members, calendar events, chores, rewards, lists and meal plans. Kinboard has no accounts, does not ask for your email or phone number, and does not collect contacts, location, photos or advertising identifiers.

Where it is stored

On your device, and — if you turn on family sync — in a database hosted by Supabase (Canada region), so that everyone with your family code sees the same board. Each family's data is reachable only with its secret 16-character family code.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data, and we use no analytics or advertising services. Anyone you give your family code to can read and change your family's board.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No reminder content is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

In the Family tab, “Delete family for everyone” permanently removes all of your family's synced data from the server. “Leave on this device” removes it from that device only. Deleting the app removes local data.

Children

Kinboard is meant to be set up and managed by parents. It collects no personal information from children beyond the names and chores parents enter.
