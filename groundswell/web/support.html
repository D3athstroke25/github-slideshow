Help & contact

Contact us

For anything this page doesn't answer, including safety concerns and questions about a pledge, send us a message. Please don't include passwords or card details.

We aim to reply within two business days, and to review reports of abusive content within 24 hours.

Backing projects

When am I charged?

Only if the project reaches its goal by its deadline. If it falls short, nothing is collected. On projects that take real payments you save a card at Stripe when you pledge, and it's charged when the project ends funded.

Can I change or cancel my pledge?

Yes, until the deadline. Open the project and tap Manage your pledge.

My card was declined

You'll get a notification and an email. Open the project and update your card within 7 days to keep your pledge and reward.

Where's my reward?

Creators deliver rewards and post progress in their updates. When a funded project is ready to ship, you'll be asked for your shipping address on the project page. Groundswell isn't a store and doesn't guarantee delivery; ask the creator in the project's comments first.

Safety

Report a project or comment

Use Report this project at the bottom of a project page, or Report under a comment. Reports are private. Moderators review every one and can remove comments, hide projects and suspend accounts.

Block someone

Tap Block on their comment or profile. You won't see their comments, they can't comment on your projects, and they can't send you notifications. Unblock them any time in Settings → Blocked accounts.

What isn't allowed

Harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, spam and anything else the Terms of Use rule out. Words that aren't allowed are filtered automatically. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

Your account

I forgot my password

On the log-in page tap Forgot password?. Use a code we email you (if you've confirmed an email address) or the recovery code you saved when you signed up.

Delete my account

Go to Settings → Delete account and enter your password. Your live projects are canceled (backers are told and not charged), your active pledges are canceled, and your profile is erased.

Creators: getting paid

Connect Stripe in Settings → Payouts. Stripe checks your identity and pays out what you raise to your bank, minus the platform fee shown there.
