Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 (reports and blocks added)

Groundswell is a crowdfunding app. This policy explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: a username, the name you show to others, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. Your recovery code is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Optional profile details: a photo, location and short bio, if you add them.
  • Your email address, only if you add one in Settings. It is used to send you notifications you can turn off, and password-reset codes. It is never shown to other people.
  • Your shipping address, only if you back a project with a reward that ships and fill in the creator's survey.
  • What you do on Groundswell: projects you create (including drafts), rewards, updates, comments, the projects you save, your pledges (amount, reward and date), and the notifications we show you.
  • Images you upload for projects, updates and your profile. These are public.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you send about projects or comments, and the people you block.
  • Your network address, briefly, to limit sign-up and log-in abuse. It is kept for at most one day.

Email and phone number are optional. We do not collect contacts or precise location, and we use no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Payments

Projects launched in test mode take no card details and move no money. Projects launched with real payments use Stripe: you enter your card on Stripe's own page, and Groundswell only stores a reference to it, plus the card brand and last four digits so you can recognize it. Creators set up payouts with Stripe directly, including identity checks. Stripe's privacy policy covers what Stripe collects.

Who can see what

  • Your name, username, photo, location, bio and launched projects are public.
  • Comments are public. Updates marked "backers only" are visible to the project's backers.
  • Creators can see the name and username of each backer of their project, the reward chosen, the pledge amount and payment status, and, after a shipping survey, the shipping address.
  • The projects you back are private unless you turn on "Show the projects I back" in Settings.
  • Drafts are private until launch, except to people you send a preview link.
  • Reports are seen only by moderators, and the person reported is not told who reported them. Only you can see who you've blocked.

Where it is stored

In a database and file store hosted by Supabase in Canada. The app itself is served by GitHub Pages. Emails are sent through Resend, and payments are processed by Stripe.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account cancels your live projects, removes your drafts, cancels your active pledges, deletes your comments' text, saved projects, notifications, shipping addresses and email, and erases your profile. Projects that already finished stay visible to their backers, credited to "Deleted user".

Children

Groundswell is not intended for children under 13.
