Who can use Groundswell

You must be at least 13 to use Groundswell. To back a project with real payments, or to launch one and receive funds, you must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding contract.

Test mode and payments

Projects launched in test mode record pledges that count toward the goal, but no payment is taken and no money changes hands. Projects launched with real payments use Stripe: backers save a card when they pledge, and it is charged only if the project reaches its goal by its deadline. If a charge fails, the backer has 7 days to update their card; after that the pledge is dropped.

Creators receive funds through their own Stripe account, less the platform fee shown when they connect Stripe. Stripe's terms also apply to creators and backers.

With real payments, the minimum pledge is $5. Backers can add an optional tip for Groundswell. A tip is charged with the pledge, only if the project is funded, goes to Groundswell rather than the creator, and doesn't count toward the goal.

If a backer's payment is refunded or disputed, the creator's share of it is taken back from the creator's Stripe account. If a dispute is decided in the creator's favor, it is paid out again.

Creators can buy a featured spot on the home page on the Groundswell website. It's paid in advance and ends on its own. It isn't refunded once it starts, except where the law requires; if the project is hidden for breaking these terms, the featuring ends without a refund.

How funding works

Projects are all-or-nothing. A project that reaches its goal by its deadline is "funded" and its pledges are collected. A project that does not reach its goal collects nothing. Backers can change or cancel a pledge until the deadline.

  • If you can't deliver a reward, tell your backers, explain what happened and how the money was used, and offer a refund to backers who haven't received what they pledged for.
