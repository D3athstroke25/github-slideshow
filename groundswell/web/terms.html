Terms of Use

Last updated: October 26, 2026 (payments added)

Test mode and payments

Projects launched in test mode record pledges that count toward the goal, but no payment is taken and no money changes hands. Projects launched with real payments use Stripe: backers save a card when they pledge, and it is charged only if the project reaches its goal by its deadline. If a charge fails, the backer has 7 days to update their card; after that the pledge is dropped.

Creators receive funds through their own Stripe account, less the platform fee shown when they connect Stripe. Stripe's terms also apply to creators and backers.

How funding works

Projects are all-or-nothing. A project that reaches its goal by its deadline is "funded" and its pledges are collected. A project that does not reach its goal collects nothing. Backers can change or cancel a pledge until the deadline.

Creators

  • You are responsible for your project and for delivering the rewards you promise.
  • Describe your project honestly, including its risks. Do not misrepresent what exists, what you can make, or who you are.
  • The funding goal, currency and duration are fixed once you launch.

Backers

Groundswell is not a store. Projects are new ideas, and some fail. Groundswell does not guarantee projects, rewards or delivery dates.

Not allowed

  • Fundraising for charity, investment, lending or equity; prizes, lotteries or gambling.
  • Weapons, drugs, illegal goods or services, or anything that breaks the law.
  • Content that harasses, threatens or impersonates others, or that you don't have the rights to.
  • Spam, fake pledges or fake accounts.

Moderators can hide projects and remove comments that break these rules. Use "Report this project" to flag a problem.

Your account

Keep your password and recovery code safe. Groundswell has no email-based recovery: without your password or recovery code, an account cannot be recovered.

Changes

These terms may change. Material changes will be announced in the app.
